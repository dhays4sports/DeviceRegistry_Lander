Device Registry

A neutral ENS namespace for device identity.

Device Registry provides a simple, human-readable way to name and reference devices on Ethereum — phones, laptops, servers, robots, and other execution environments — without binding them to a specific wallet, vendor, or application.

Built on ENS, the registry makes device identity explicit, composable, and revocable. It defines no behavior and enforces no policy.

The registry exists solely to provide a shared naming layer for devices that execute actions on behalf of humans, organizations, or software systems.

Examples

phone.device.eth
laptop.device.eth
robot.device.eth
server.device.eth

Examples are illustrative only. Interpretation is left to consuming systems.

Status

Experimental Opt-in Naming-only

Non-Goals

Device Registry does not define standards, replace vendor device management, enforce permissions or security models, or claim authority over implementations.

The registry exists to name devices — nothing more.
